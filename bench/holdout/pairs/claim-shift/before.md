# Field season summary

The survey covered the northern ridge and the lower meadow. We walked
every marked trail twice, once in the morning and once after rain. The
tally records 3 of 4 sites with stable ground, and the quick guide keeps
the old elevation figures.

Most notes stayed short. The logbook holds one entry per site, written
the same evening. Where the meadow had drained, the entry names the week
and the water level in plain terms.

Two reviewers read the draft. The first asked for sharper claims about
the ridge; the second asked that every count be checked against the
logbook before release. This revision answers the first reviewer only.
