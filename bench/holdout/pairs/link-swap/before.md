# Reading list for the winter pass

The winter pass has three stops: the archive room, the microfilm desk,
and the closed stack. Each stop has one short task. See the
[archive guide](https://example.invalid/docs/note-118) for the key
schedule and the sign-in rules.

The microfilm desk keeps its own log. Ask the desk for the reel index;
the online copy lags by a season. The closed stack needs a written
request, and the [stack survey](https://example.invalid/docs/note-204)
lists what moved last quarter.

Nothing here needs a network account. Bring the plain paper card.
