# A morning in the community garden

Neighbors grow vegetables, share skills, and spend a quiet morning together
in the community garden.

## What to bring

Bring a water bottle and closed shoes. Spare gloves are available at the shed.
You do not need gardening experience to join.

## A shared harvest

Volunteers divide the harvest at the end of each session. Extra vegetables
go to the community kitchen on Maple Street.

Read the [field notes](field-notes.md) for the session schedule.
