Tmux demonstration: draft changes (Git) and reviewed documents (Margin).

LEFT PANE
$ git diff start draft
diff --git a/article.md b/article.md
index 2ebd0f7..f604ca4 100644
--- a/article.md
+++ b/article.md
@@ -2,4 +2,3 @@

-The garden is a place where neighbors
-can spend some time together while
-they grow vegetables and flowers.
+Neighbors grow food, share skills,
+and spend a quiet morning together.
diff --git a/notes.md b/notes.md
index f748cb5..ac0c4cc 100644
--- a/notes.md
+++ b/notes.md
@@ -2,3 +2,3 @@

-- Meet at the north gate at 09:00.
-- Give the tomatoes 12 liters of water.
+- Meet at the north gate at 10:00.
+- Give the tomatoes 20 liters of water.

$

RIGHT PANE
$ margin show --path article.md --revision HEAD \
  --format raw --allow-tty
# A morning in the garden

Neighbors grow food, share skills,
and spend a quiet morning together.

$ margin show --path notes.md --revision HEAD \
  --format raw --allow-tty
# Saturday session

- Meet at the north gate at 09:00.
- Give the tomatoes 12 liters of water.

$ margin status --json | \
  jq -C '.data | {preservation, review}'
{
  "preservation": "captured",
  "review": "reviewed"
}

$
